WiFi Speed Booster

Privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026

In short: WiFi Speed Booster has no account and no server. Your speed test history is saved only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Location permission

Android only lets apps see your Wi-Fi name and the networks around you if they have the Location permission. The app uses it only for that. It never reads your GPS position, and nothing about your location is stored or sent anywhere.

What the app reads on your phone

Your Wi-Fi name, signal strength, link speed, band and channel, your router's local address, your phone's local IP and DNS settings, and the names, signal and channels of nearby Wi-Fi networks. These are used on the phone to find what is slowing your Wi-Fi and are not uploaded.

What the app stores

  • Your speed test results (speed, delay, time and Wi-Fi name), up to the last 50
  • Your internet plan speed, if you enter it

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

Internet use

  • Speed test: the app downloads and uploads test data with Cloudflare's public speed test service (speed.cloudflare.com). Like any website, Cloudflare sees your public IP address for these requests, under its own privacy policy. No personal data is sent.
  • Router check: the app briefly connects to your own router on your home network to measure its delay. This never leaves your home.
  • Router settings: the "Router settings" button opens your router's page in your browser.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Tap Clear on the speed test history. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
