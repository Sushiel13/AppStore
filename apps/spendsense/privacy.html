SpendSense

Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

In short: SpendSense reads transaction SMS on your phone to track your spending. It has no internet access, no account and no server, so your messages and expenses never leave your phone.

SMS access

With your permission, SpendSense reads SMS in your inbox and new SMS as they arrive. It only keeps messages that look like bank, UPI or card transactions from business senders (for example HDFCBK or SBIUPI). Messages from personal phone numbers, OTPs, payment requests and reminders are skipped and not stored.

What the app stores

  • For each transaction: date, amount, spend or credit, merchant name, category, payment mode, bank, the last 4 digits of the account or card, any note you add, and the text of that transaction SMS
  • Categories you set for merchants
  • Transactions you add by hand

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not backed up to the cloud by the app.

Internet use

None. The app doesn't ask for internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or anyone else.

Exporting

If you tap Export, the app writes a CSV file to the place you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Delete any transaction in the app. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. You can turn off SMS access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → SpendSense → Permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
