House Help Tracker

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

In short: the app has no account and no server. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We don't collect, see, sell or share any of it.

What the app stores

To work, the app saves the details you type in, on your device only:

  • Names, work, mobile numbers, UPI IDs, joining and exit dates of the people you employ
  • Photos you choose for them, resized and kept inside the app
  • Attendance, leave notes, pay type and amounts, bonuses, advances and payment records
  • Household items, their categories, barcodes and expiry dates
  • Your settings, such as your name on messages, WhatsApp numbers for alerts, language, theme and an optional app-lock PIN (stored only as a one-way hash)

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Messages on WhatsApp and SMS

When you choose to send a leave message, pay slip, monthly report or expiry alert, the app opens WhatsApp or your SMS app with the message filled in. Nothing is sent until you tap send in that app. Those apps handle the message under their own privacy policies.

Paying with UPI

When you tap Pay, the app opens a UPI app on your phone (for example Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm) with the name, UPI ID and amount filled in, or opens the UPI app you pick so you can pay to a mobile number. The payment happens entirely inside that app. The app never sees or stores bank details, and it records a payment only when you tap Paid.

Free trial and Pro

The app keeps, on your phone only, the date your free trial started, a random phone ID (like HHT-7KQ2-M9XA) and your Pro unlock code. They are not linked to your name or number and are not sent anywhere by the app.

To get Pro, you pay by UPI in your own UPI app and choose to send a WhatsApp message with your plan, phone ID and UPI reference to the developer. The developer uses these only to check your payment and send your unlock code. The unlock code is checked on your phone, without the internet.

Scanning a pack

When you tap Scan, the app uses a photo you take or choose to read the product name or expiry date. The text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit, provided through Google Play services. The photo is not uploaded to us and is not kept after the text is read.

Google ML Kit may send Google basic diagnostic information, such as device model, Android version, app version, performance and error data, and a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you. Google uses it for diagnostics and usage analytics under its own terms and privacy policy. It does not include your photos or records.

Scanning a barcode

If you scan a barcode, the app uses Google's code scanner in Google Play services. To suggest a product name, it sends only the barcode number to the public Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts databases. No other information is sent with it.

Voice input

If you tap the microphone to say a product name or date, the app opens your phone's speech recognition (usually provided by Google). What you say is handled by that service under its own privacy policy, and only the recognised text comes back to the app. The app does not record or keep any audio.

Fingerprint unlock

If you turn on fingerprint unlock, Android checks your fingerprint. The app only learns whether the check passed and never receives fingerprint data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app checks your household items once a day on your phone and reminds you 7 days before and on the expiry day. This check runs entirely on the device.

Internet use

Besides the barcode lookup above, the app connects to the internet only to load its font from Google Fonts. No personal data is included in these requests.

Backups

If you save or share a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to the place you choose (for example your phone's storage or Google Drive). We never receive it.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools of its own.

Deleting your data

Remove a person or an item inside the app at any time. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing their household and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
