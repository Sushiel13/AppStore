Photo & Video Settings Guide

Privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026 (version 2.2)

In short: Photo & Video Settings Guide shows you which camera settings to use for a scene. It asks for no permissions, has no internet access, no account and no server, so nothing about you leaves your phone.

What the app stores

  • The phone model you pick, so you don't have to choose it again
  • Your last Photo or Video choice

This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

Camera and photos

The app doesn't use the camera and can't see your photos or videos. The Open Camera button opens your phone's own camera app.

Internet use

None. The app doesn't ask for internet permission, so it cannot send anything to us or anyone else.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
