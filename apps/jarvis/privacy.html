Jarvis

Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

In short: Jarvis has no account and no server. Your notes, lists, reminders and chats are saved only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

What Jarvis does with your requests

Jarvis understands your requests on your phone and carries them out with your phone's own apps: the clock app for alarms and timers, the phone and messaging apps for calls and texts, WhatsApp, maps and your browser. Calls and messages are only prepared; you tap call or send yourself.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the orb, Jarvis listens using your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's). That service turns your words into text under its own privacy policy. Jarvis doesn't record or keep audio. Replies are read out by your phone's text-to-speech.

Contacts

If you ask Jarvis to call or message someone by name, it reads your contacts on the phone to find their number. Your contacts are never uploaded or stored by Jarvis.

What the app stores

  • Your notes, lists (for example shopping or to-do) and upcoming reminders
  • Your recent chat with Jarvis (the last 80 messages)
  • Your settings: name, home city, country calling code, voice options and, if you add one, your AI key

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

Internet use

  • Weather: the city name you ask about is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the forecast. No other data is sent.
  • Smart answers (optional, off by default): if you add an AI key in Settings, questions Jarvis can't handle itself, with your recent messages for context, are sent to the service you choose (for example Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or a server on your own computer). That service handles them under its own privacy policy, using your own account.
  • Search, YouTube and maps: these open in your browser or the matching app, which handle them under their own policies.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Clear your chat in Settings, or say "Clear my notes", "Clear my shopping list" or "Cancel my reminders". To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
