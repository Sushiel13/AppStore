AppBucket app privacy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

What the app does

AppBucket shows the apps on appbucket.in and installs the ones you choose. It downloads app files only from appbucket.in, over a secure connection, and opens Android's installer so you approve every install.

Data

  • No sign-up, no account, and no personal data is collected.
  • To show Open or Update, the app checks on your phone which AppBucket apps are installed. This list is never sent anywhere.
  • About twice a day the app downloads the public app list from appbucket.in to see if an update is out, and shows a notification if so.
  • No ads and no analytics.

Permissions

  • Install apps: to install the apps you choose.
  • See installed apps: to show Open or Update.
  • Notifications: for update alerts. You can turn them off in Android settings.
  • Internet: to load the store and download apps.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on appbucket.in.
